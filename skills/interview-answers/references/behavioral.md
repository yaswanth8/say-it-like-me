# Behavioral, intro and HR answers

## "Tell me about a time..."

Spoken shape, about 60 seconds, five to seven sentences:

1. **Situation** in one or two sentences: where, what was at stake. Name the company, client or domain only if the profile does.
2. **What I did**, the longest part: decisions and actions, with "I". Use "we" only for things the team did together.
3. **Result**: what changed. Words, or a real number from the user. Never an invented one.
4. Optionally one sentence on what I'd keep doing. Not a slogan.

No "Situation / Task / Action / Result" labels in the answer; the structure should be felt, not announced.

### Where the stories come from

1. The profile's `stories`. Pick the one whose `useFor` matches the question; one story can answer several questions from different angles.
2. Otherwise, the roles and highlights in the profile (projects, migrations, launches, incidents, tools introduced). Build the closest true story and add one sentence after it naming what to swap for the real details.

### Common questions and what they're really asking

| Question | What they want to hear |
|---|---|
| Tell me about yourself | 60-90 seconds: current role and what I own, two highlights that match this job, why I'm looking at this role. Not the whole resume in order. |
| Hardest problem you solved | How I reason and debug, not how hard it was. |
| Production incident / crisis | Calm triage, finding the root cause, the fix that stops it recurring. |
| Disagreement / conflict | Disagreeing with data and respect, and committing once a decision is made. |
| Tight deadline | Cutting scope deliberately and communicating early. |
| Mistake / failure | Owning it plainly, the fix, what changed after. A real but survivable mistake. |
| Weakness | A real one with what I'm doing about it. Not "I'm a perfectionist". |
| Mentoring / leadership | Knowledge transfer, reviews, unblocking others. |
| Working with non-technical people | Translating, choosing what matters to them, checking understanding. |
| Why are you leaving / why this company | Forward-looking: what I want to do more of. Never negative about the current employer. |

## Intro ("Walk me through your background")

> Current title and years, the main skills in one breath, the current project and what I own on it, one earlier role in one sentence, and why this role fits.

Use the profile's roles and stated years. A gap in employment gets one neutral sentence, and only if the profile explains it.

## HR and screening

Answer from the profile's `quick` answers in one or two sentences: work authorization, sponsorship, notice period, start date, relocation, remote / hybrid, background check. When an answer is "Not set" (salary often is), don't make up a figure: give a way to answer without a number ("I'm looking for something in line with the market for this role; what range did you have budgeted?"), and ask the user once whether to save a real one.
